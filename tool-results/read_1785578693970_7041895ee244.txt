    65→    65→  * Rip-up and retry: when direct routing fails, finds blocking traces (bbox intersection with source→target), removes them one by one, re-runs A*, then re-routes the ripped-up trace with the new trace as an obstacle.
    66→    66→  * Sorted blocking traces by length (shortest first) to minimize disruption.
    67→    67→  * Path simplification: removes collinear points to keep the route clean.
